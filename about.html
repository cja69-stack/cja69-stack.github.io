Personal background

My name is Charlie Acosta, and I spent my earliest years in Ecuador. When I was two years old, I moved to the United States, where I grew up and continued my schooling. My story connects those early years in Ecuador with the life I have built in the United States.

I attended Lafayette Street School for middle school, followed by Newark School of Data Science and Information Technology from 2024 to 2026. I am now attending New Jersey Institute of Technology, with my studies spanning 2026 to 2030 (expected). Alongside my coursework, this portfolio gives me a place to share projects and document what I am learning.

Outside the classroom, I enjoy playing soccer, cooking, and hanging out with friends. Staying active and exploring new things are part of how I like to spend my time. Mechanical engineering is another interest of mine, alongside the work and learning experiences you can explore here.

This website is a personal introduction as well as a collection of my work. It brings together my background, the things I enjoy, and projects from different stages of my learning. As you look around, you can get to know both the person behind the pages and the work I am sharing along the way.
